# THE OVERSEER SURFACE — the founder's twelve answers

Session 51, 1 Sep 2026. The overseer-dashboard conversation `SPEC §16` opened
in session 50 is now had: the proposal (the Overseer Surface artifact, drawn
three times over — empty, real, overloaded — from the seeded database) asked
twelve questions, and the founder answered all twelve. His answers are
recorded here in the shape `28-fixation/02-founder-answers.md` set; each names
the rule work it produces. Where an answer needs no rule, that is said rather
than a rule invented.

The build order the answers land in: the answers themselves (this file and
the rule edits), lead source made mandatory (small, early, so real data
starts arriving filled), then the Today tab built to the approved drawings.
Reports and Team are later sessions — the founder uses Today for a week
first.

## 1 · "Coming back" → NEW WORK ONLY

Only when they buy again on NEW work. A second delivery on the same job is
one deal finishing, not a customer returning.

→ **New S139 [BUILD]** — a company has come back when its approved
dispatches span more than one project. Derived from dispatches, never
flagged. The chart reads it in the Reports session.

## 2 · "A real customer" → BOUGHT AT LEAST ONCE, EVER

Bought at least once, ever — 36 of 382 today. Revisit after a year of real
data.

→ **New S140 [BUILD]** — a real customer is a company with at least one
approved dispatch, ever. The revisit is recorded in `SPEC §16`.

## 3 · "Is his pipeline real?" → QUOTED-VERSUS-DELIVERED, NEVER A SCORE

His record of quoted-versus-delivered convinces. Show how long deals have
been sitting beside it; never combine the two into a score.

→ **New S142 [BUILD]** — the two figures render side by side and no screen
may fold them into one number. This is `§16`'s "nothing scores a rep" made a
citable rule, and it is the second thing `D43`'s by-rep bars gain when the
Reports tab is built: the sitting time beside the conversion.

## 4 · "A big deal" → THE BIGGEST FIVE

The biggest five by expected size. The real spread runs 3,100 down to
2,400 m², so a fixed threshold would show all or none.

→ Carried inside **new D80 [BUILD]** — *Big deals in play* is the top five
open projects by expected square metres. No threshold exists anywhere.

## 5 · Quoting "how much" → A COUNT, NEVER THE METRES

A count of deals. Never the metre figure — a number that inflates is a
number that lies.

→ **No new rule.** `S68` already forbids summing quotations and `D32`
already made the dashboard's side figure a count of threads. The Today
band's *quotation threads open* figure cites both; nothing is added.

## 6 · "Gone silent" → A NUMBER OF DAYS, ADJUSTABLE

A number of days, adjustable by super admin, starting at 2–3 weeks. Not
"this week" — that fires on a Monday for everyone.

→ **New S141** — a settings row (`attention.silent_days`, seeded 14 — the
near end of his 2–3 weeks; the screen to edit it is the same missing half as
the quiet thresholds, `§4` row 37). Read at query time by *Who needs
attention* `D79`.

## 7 · "Yesterday" on a Sunday → THURSDAY ALWAYS, SATURDAY ONLY IF REAL

Thursday ALWAYS, plus Saturday only if something happened. Saturday is not a
working day and nobody is expected to work it, but reps outside Riyadh or
working from home sometimes do, and that work should show. Never render an
empty Saturday section.

→ Carried inside **new D77 [BUILD]** — *Yesterday* reads the previous
working day; on a Sunday a Saturday section renders exactly when a Saturday
figure is non-zero. Friday never renders — his answer named Saturday alone.

## 8 · Why we lose → RANKED BY HOW OFTEN

By how often a reason comes up, not by metres — with the metres beside each
reason. One big deal lost on price is bad luck; five lost on lead time is a
problem.

→ **D44 amended [BUILD]** — the loss bars rank by count, square metres
printed beside each. On today's real data the two rankings disagree
(price leads by metres from one deal; "other" leads by count with five),
which is what the answer decides between.

## 9 · "Untouched too long" → THE NUMBER WITH ITS BREAKDOWN

The number WITH its breakdown beneath it. 191 alone is not actionable; 102
stalled projects and 66 quiet companies are different jobs chased
differently.

→ Carried inside **new D78 [BUILD]** — the *Stuck* block's untouched figure
always renders its per-anchor breakdown line.

## 10 · Companies with no lead source → THE FIELD BECOMES MANDATORY

A RULE CHANGE, not a chart decision: lead source becomes required when a rep
adds a company. The existing 261 blanks stay untouched — nothing invents
sources for them. The chart shows "not recorded" as its own bar until they
age out; what to do with the 261 is a founder decision after the pilot.

→ **S17 amended [CHANGE]** — mandatory on creation; a company already blank
may stay blank on edit (forcing a value at edit time would invent the very
data the blank honestly records). The "not recorded" bar lands with the
Reports tab; the 261 decision is a new `§16` open item.

## 11 · The first day → A FIRST-RUN SCREEN

Zeros on day one look broken to a rep who has never seen it working, and
first impressions decide whether they use it.

→ **New D81 [BUILD]** — while the system has never recorded any work at all
(no report, no quotation thread, no dispatch — three empty tables, checked
at read time), the overseer's Today renders a first-run screen naming what
to do first instead of a row of zeros. The moment any of the three exists,
the real blocks take over.

## 12 · A rep with no target → APPEARS, WITH A DASH

He appears on the team table, with all his real figures and a dash where the
target and pace bar would be. The only difference between a rep with a
target and one without is whether achievement can be measured against
anything; everything else he does still happens and management still needs
to see it. Majed holding 14 companies unseen is what absence caused.

→ **D39 amended [CHANGE]** — the row set becomes every active visible
book-holder the reader may see, targeted or not; the dash is the only thing
a missing target changes. Built with the Team tab session, which is where
the table now lives.

## What the conversation settles beyond the twelve

- **The overseer surface itself** — one dashboard, three tabs (Today ·
  Reports · Team), the tab strip as a group of `D64`'s flag-qualified
  blocks. New rules `D76`–`D81`; `D64` rewritten; `D68` updated. `SPEC §16`'s
  open item closes: the conversation is had, and this file is its record.
- **`D64`'s book-holder narrowing ships with the Today tab** — the approved
  drawings have no waiting list, counts strip or greeting on an overseer's
  screen, so `§4` row 52's core lands in the build session rather than on
  its own.
- **`S138`'s manager count finds its place** — a per-rep never-contacted
  figure surfaces on *Who needs attention* `D79` when it is large, and as a
  Team-table column when that tab is built.
- **The tab strip carries no Reports pill until the Reports tab exists**
  (`D51` — a control that does nothing is worse than no control), and
  **Targets stays in the rail until the Team tab absorbs it** (`28b`'s
  precedent: moving the rail item first hides attainment from everyone who
  could reach it).
